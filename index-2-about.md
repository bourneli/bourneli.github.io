### About Me

Originally from Wuhan, Hubei, I relocated to Shenzhen at age 23 to begin my career at Tencent. Over a decade at Tencent’s Shenzhen headquarters, I served as a Senior Researcher in Applied Research—though at heart, I have always been a dedicated Machine Learning Engineer. Known colloquially among teammates as "2rd Bro" (a nod to my *Honor of Kings* gameplay with Guan Yu), I pride myself on applying technical rigor to complex challenges.

In September 2021, I embarked on an international expansion path, moving to Singapore as a pioneer exploring global game markets for Tencent’s Interactive Entertainment Group (IEG Global). In August 2025, I relocated to the UK to further drive our expansion across the European market. Having established market presence across two major international hubs, this global path has uniquely defined my professional trajectory.
